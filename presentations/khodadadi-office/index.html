01 / SPACE & IDENTITY

Meeting room

Public access from waiting. Concealed access from the manager’s office. A composed digital presence.

MEETING ROOM / CONCEPT

Public access

Normal visitor access runs from the waiting room directly into the meeting room.

Visitors enter from the waiting room. The manager uses a separate concealed doorway from the manager’s office.

MEETING ROOM / CONCEPT

Concealed manager access

A concealed doorway connects the manager’s office directly to the meeting room.

This hidden connection is reserved for the manager. Normal visitor access is from the waiting room to the meeting room.

MEETING ROOM / CONCEPT

Controlled visibility

The suspended mirror stops above the floor, leaving a low strip open.

The approximately 50 cm opening makes occupancy legible at floor level while screening seated eye-level views.

MEETING ROOM / CONCEPT

Reflection & presence

The mirror extends visual depth by reflecting people and the room.

Reflection is a conceptual expression of openness and mutual presence. The mirror remains opaque; it is not transparent glass.

MEETING ROOM / CONCEPT

The online frame

The head seat is composed with ATLAS and greenery behind it.

For an energy company, light and planting evoke life, renewal and movement. They give the brand a living backdrop rather than a neutral wall.

MEETING ROOM / CONCEPT

The planted edge

Layered planting and filtered glass soften the boundary and daylight.

Greenery supports the energy-company narrative through light and living growth. This is a spatial and symbolic intention, not a claim of energy performance.
