Jalal Mashhadi Fard

One mind,
many forms.

Architect, creative director, fashion designer and visual artist working across Iran, Oman and the UAE.

Born in Mashhad in 1993 and educated in architecture and related fields at Tehran University of Art, Jalal Mashhadi Fard builds a multidisciplinary practice across architecture, objects, garments, images and exhibitions.

His work draws on lived intercultural experience and on concepts including love, redemption and the search for truth. Through light, reflection, material, symbolism and narrative, he creates poetic connections between Eastern and Western traditions and between matter and meaning.
