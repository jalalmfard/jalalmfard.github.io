Jalal Mashhadi Fard

One mind,
many forms.

Jalal Mashhadi Fard is an architect, creative director, fashion designer, and visual artist working across Iran, Oman, and the UAE. His multidisciplinary practice moves between architecture, objects, images, exhibitions, and identity—treating each medium as part of one continuous spatial language.

Through light, reflection, material, and narrative, his work explores how space can hold memory, intimacy, encounter, and emotion.
